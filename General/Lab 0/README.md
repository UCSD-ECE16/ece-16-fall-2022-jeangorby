# Tutorial Review
## Tutorial 1: Design Thinking
In this tutorial, we learned about the process of design thinking. The design process consists of defining a problem, designing a solution, and then building and testing all the while reiterating through these steps until a satisfying solution is met. It is important that all these steps are followed elaborately, otherwise, problems may be encountered. It is of utmost important that the needs of the users are fully understood in order to really solve problems rather than just implementing interesting solutions.
